About Seventeen

Seventeen (세븐틴) is a popular South Korean boy band formed by Pledis Entertainment that debuted on May 26, 2015. Despite their name, the group consists of 13 members divided into three specialized sub-units: the Hip-Hop Team, Vocal Team, and Performance Team.


Group Meaning

  • Name
    Represents 13 members + 3 sub-units + 1 unified team (13 + 3 + 1 = 17).
  • Fandom Name
    Carat (캐럿), representing how the fans make the group shine brighter.

The 13 members by unit

  • Hip-Hop Team S.Coups (leader), Wonwoo, Mingyu, Vernon.
  • Vocal TeamWoozi (leader), Jeonghan, Joshua, DK, Seungkwan.
  • Performance TeamHoshi (leader), Jun, The8, Dino.
